\chapter{Traceability Matrix}\label{app:traceability}

This appendix traces every claim in the thesis from motivation to evidence. To structure that trace,
the evaluation is organized around four questions: (RQ1) is a key substitution detected, in band,
once monitoring begins? (RQ2) does adding that detection avoid enlarging an individual lookup proof,
and what is its verification cost? (RQ3) is offline catch-up bounded independently of the number of
missed epochs? and (RQ4) can the client verifier integrate into a real messaging-client codebase? Each
is linked below to the design claim it makes, the mechanism that delivers it, the test or scenario that
checks it, and the benchmark that measures it. \Cref{tab:trace-rq} traces each question
from its design claim to the supporting evidence; \cref{tab:trace-contrib} organizes the same evidence
by the four contributions of \cref{sec:intro-contributions}.
