\chapter{Traceability Matrix}\label{app:traceability}

This appendix links the research questions of \cref{sec:intro-aims} to the design claim each makes,
the mechanism that delivers it, the test or scenario that checks it, and the benchmark that measures
it, so that every claim in the thesis can be traced from motivation to evidence. \Cref{tab:trace-rq}
gives the research-question view; \cref{tab:trace-contrib} maps the four contribution kinds to their
strongest supporting evidence.
